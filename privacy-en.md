# Arielzinha Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 3, 2025

## Summary
Your privacy is fundamental to us. This policy transparently explains how your data is handled.

*   **What do we collect?** Only what's necessary for the bot to function: Discord IDs (user, server, channels), server settings, and commands you run.
*   **Why do we collect it?** To provide and improve the bot's features, save your preferences, and fix issues.
*   **Is your data sold?** Never. We do not sell, rent, or use your data for marketing.
*   **Who do we share it with?** Only with essential service providers (like our bot host) or if we are compelled by law.
*   **What are your rights?** You can request to access or delete your data at any time.

---

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the Privacy Policy for **Arielzinha** ("Bot"). This document details what information we collect, how we use it, and the measures we take to protect it. This policy applies to all users who interact with the Bot on any Discord server.

By adding or using Arielzinha, you consent to the practices described in this document.

## 2. The Information We Collect
We collect only the data strictly necessary for the Bot's operation. It is divided into two categories:

#### a) Data You Provide Directly
*   **Server Settings:** Information that administrators configure, such as a command prefix, designated music channels, DJ roles, and other server-specific preferences.

#### b) Data Collected Automatically (Usage Data)
*   **Discord Identifiers (IDs):** User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, and Channel IDs are collected to execute commands correctly, apply settings, and manage permissions.
*   **Command Data:** The content of the commands you execute (e.g., `!play <song link>`). This data is processed to fulfill your request and is not permanently stored in our database.
*   **Technical Logs:** Diagnostic and error logs, which may include IDs and commands, are used to monitor stability, identify bugs, and ensure the security of the service. These logs are rotational and periodically deleted.

#### c) What We DO NOT Collect
We do **NOT** collect, store, or request:
*   Your Discord email address or password.
*   Any message content in servers that are not direct commands to the Bot.
*   Sensitive personal information such as your real name, address, or age.

## 3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Data Processing
We use your data for specific purposes and with a clear legal basis.

| Data Type | Purpose of Use | Legal Basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| IDs (User, Server, Channel) | To operate the Bot, apply settings and permissions. | **Performance of a Service** |
| Server Settings | To customize the Bot's experience on your server. | **Performance of a Service** |
| Commands & Interactions | To process your requests (play music, skip, etc.). | **Performance of a Service** |
| Technical Logs | To diagnose errors, monitor performance, and prevent abuse. | **Legitimate Interest** |

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
Our users' trust is our priority. We do not sell or rent your data. Sharing is restricted to the following situations:

*   **Hosting Providers:** The Bot and its database are hosted on third-party platforms (cloud services). These providers have access to the stored data but are contractually obligated to keep it secure and confidential.
*   **Legal Obligation:** We may disclose your information if required by law, subpoena, or other valid legal process, or to protect our rights, property, and safety.

## 5. Data Security and Retention
*   **Security:** We implement technical and administrative security measures to protect your data, including strict access control and encryption for data in transit and at rest. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
*   **Retention:** We retain data only for as long as necessary:
    *   **Server Settings:** These are kept as long as the Bot remains on your server. When the Bot is removed, this data is flagged for deletion.
    *   **Technical Logs:** These are automatically deleted on a regular cycle (typically 7 to 30 days).

## 6. Your Privacy Rights
As a user, you have control over your information. Your rights include:

*   **Right of Access:** You can request a copy of the data we hold associated with your server or user.
*   **Right to Rectification:** If any information is incorrect, you can request its correction.
*   **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** You can request the deletion of your data. The simplest way to do this for server data is to remove the Bot from the server. For individual requests, please contact us.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via our support server.

## 7. Children's Privacy
Our service is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement (currently 13 years or older). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child has provided us with data, we will take steps to remove it from our systems.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through our support server or other official channels. Your continued use of the Bot after the update date constitutes your acceptance of the new terms.

## 9. Contact
For any questions, concerns, or to exercise your privacy rights, the best way to contact us is through our [official Discord server](https://dcserver.link/brasilpokedrink).
